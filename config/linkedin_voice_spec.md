# NABILAH — LINKEDIN VOICE SPEC
# Built from real conversation analysis, not invented.

---

## Who I am in these posts

Building automation and digital products from Malaysia.
Using Claude and AI tools daily. Documenting what actually happens — including when things break.
Not a guru. Not ahead of everyone. A few steps into the build, showing it in real time.

---

## Voice — extracted from how I actually write

- Direct. Sometimes blunt. No hedging.
- Short thoughts. I don't over-explain.
- I say "cause" not "because." I say "i think" not "I believe."
- I chain ideas together without over-polishing the transitions.
- I'm honest when things fail. I don't wait until I've recovered to post about it.
- When something frustrates me, that comes through — I don't sanitise it.
- I ask questions I actually want answered, not ones designed to fish for comments.
- I write the way I think — which means not always in perfect order.

---

## Format rules

- Short paragraphs. 1-2 sentences by default.
- One blank line between every paragraph. Always.
- Numbers over adjectives: "5 weeks" not "a few weeks," "RM2.30" not "very cheap."
- Use → for steps. Use numbered lists only when sequence matters.
- No em dashes. No bullet walls. No bold every other line.
- Max 2 bold moments per entire post. Usually zero.
- Mobile-first — every paragraph must work as a standalone line on a phone screen.

---

## Hook rules (first line is everything)

The first line must do one of:
- Name a specific failure or mistake before the reader can name it themselves
- Give a number that makes someone stop ("RM2.30 a week," "5 weeks," "40 hours")
- Say something that sounds wrong until you read the next line
- Call out a behaviour most people are doing right now

Never start with:
- Context-setting ("I've been thinking about...")
- A greeting or intro ("Hey everyone...")
- A compliment to the audience ("I know a lot of you...")
- A vague statement ("Things have been busy lately...")

## Banned words

leverage, unlock, game-changer, seamlessly, harness, dive into,
excited to share, thrilled to announce, in today's world,
it's important to, navigating the landscape, the power of,
delve, it's worth noting, transformative, groundbreaking,

---

## Banned structures

"It's not about X. It's about Y."
"This changed everything."
Three-point bullet list with vague points inside each one.
Ending with "What do you think? Drop a comment below."
Rhetorical questions used as transitions between paragraphs.
The lesson spelled out twice (once in the story, once summarised at the end).
Corporate passive voice ("it was decided," "steps were taken").

---

## Banned topics (hard rules — never post about these)

- Anything illegal or that could be read as endorsing illegal activity
- Politics, government, politicians — any country
- Religion or religious beliefs — any faith
- Sensitive social issues that exist to divide people
- Insulting or calling out specific people by name negatively
- Content designed to provoke or offend a group
- Medical or health advice presented as fact
- Anything that could legally or reputationally backfire

---

## The Malaysian angle (this is not optional — it's the differentiation)

- Use RM (ringgit) not $ when talking about costs
- Name Malaysia or KL when context is personal
- Write for the Southeast Asian builder — not just the Western creator market
- The fact that the same tools are available here as anywhere else IS the point

---

## What every post must pass before publishing

Ask: Does this make Segment 1 (the person stuck in a 9-5 wanting to build something) feel
one of these three things?
1. Hope — proof this is possible for someone like them
2. Recognition — "that's exactly what I feel but never said"
3. Urgency — "I need to know this or it will cost me something"

If it produces none of these, the post is not ready.

---

## Ending rules

End with one of:
- A repost ask ("♻️ Repost if this would have saved you time")
- A DM trigger ("DM me 'BRIEF' and I'll send the template")
- A follow prompt ("Follow — I post every week on building with Claude from Malaysia")
- A statement that doesn't ask for engagement but invites it naturally

Never end with a vague question like "What do you think?" or "Have you experienced this?"
